# Typography

Fonts and text styles belong to the theme: switch **Theme** in the toolbar to compare.

## Text styles

Text styles bundle family, size, line height, weight, letter spacing and case into one
utility — `text-heading-1`, `text-body`, `text-caption`, … — and are what `OHeading` and
`OText` use. Prefer them over combining `text-*`, `leading-*` and `font-*` by hand.

<div
  style={{
    display: 'grid',
    gridTemplateColumns: 'max-content 1fr',
    gap: '1.25rem 2rem',
    alignItems: 'baseline',
    padding: '1.5rem',
    borderRadius: '0.75rem',
    background: 'var(--oct-color-surface)',
    color: 'var(--oct-color-fg)',
    border: '1px solid var(--oct-color-border)',
  }}
>
  {textStyleNames.map((name) => [
    <code key={`${name}-name`} style={{ fontSize: '0.75rem', color: 'var(--oct-color-fg-muted)' }}>
      text-{name}
    </code>,
    <span
      key={`${name}-sample`}
      style={{
        fontFamily: `var(--oct-text-${name}-family)`,
        fontSize: `var(--oct-text-${name}-size)`,
        fontWeight: `var(--oct-text-${name}-weight)`,
        lineHeight: `var(--oct-text-${name}-leading)`,
        letterSpacing: `var(--oct-text-${name}-tracking)`,
        textTransform: `var(--oct-text-${name}-transform)`,
      }}
    >
      The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog
    </span>,
  ])}
</div>
